# launch

## What it does

Gets a product ready to be seen: page audit, share card from source, demo media, channel-specific copy, a submission tracker and a launch-week readout.

## When to reach for it

Product Hunt, Show HN, X or directory launches, or before telling the public about anything new.

## It's working if

page-audit.mjs shows no errors on every shared URL, the share card renders at 1200x630, every claim in the copy is sourced, and the tracker lists each destination with status.

## Common questions

**Will it post or submit for me?** It drafts everything. Posting and submissions happen under your accounts with your go-ahead per destination.
