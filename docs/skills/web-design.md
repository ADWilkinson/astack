# web-design

## What it does

Gives an interface a direction that fits the product, holds a craft floor while building, animates with purpose, and checks the render at 390 and 1440 in light and dark. Keeps a DESIGN.md so later sessions do not drift back to defaults, and checks its plan against today's AI-design defaults before building.

## When to reach for it

New pages, redesigns, "this looks AI-generated", brand refreshes, or any change where hierarchy, type, colour or mobile layout matter.

## It's working if

The screenshots show a page you could recognise without its logo, no overflow or stranded words, real content in every state, and DESIGN.md records the decisions.

## Common questions

**Will it impose a house style?** No. Your brief and DESIGN.md win. The anti-pattern list is a set of defaults to question, not a ban list.

**Does it need Chrome?** For screenshots, yes: shoot.mjs drives a local Chrome or Chromium.
