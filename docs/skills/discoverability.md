# discoverability

## What it does

Audits what search and AI crawlers get from a site, fixes the cause, and measures a frozen query cohort before and after.

## When to reach for it

Indexing problems, "why doesn't ChatGPT mention us", comparison and alternatives pages, before and after a content push.

## It's working if

page-audit.mjs reports no errors on the target pages, the cohort and baseline are written down, and the readout compares valid runs over equal windows.

## Common questions

**Is there a magic AI-search file?** No. Ordinary SEO eligibility and useful, specific content are the foundation.
