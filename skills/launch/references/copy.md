## The pieces

| Piece | Length | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tagline | ≤ 60 characters | What it is and for whom. Used in directories and cards. |
| Short description | 1–2 sentences | Tagline plus the one detail that makes it worth a click. |
| Long description | 100–200 words | What it does, how it works in a sentence, who it is for, what it costs, the link. |
| Social post | 1–4 short lines + link | One claim, one proof (a number, a clip, a screenshot), the link. |
| Community post | Platform norms | Story or technical angle; why you built it; what you want feedback on. |
| Changelog | One line per change | Past tense, user-visible changes first, link to details. |

## Angle by audience

Write different first lines for different readers; the product is the same, the reason to care is not.

- **Users of the problem**: the outcome ("Book a vet appointment in two taps").
- **Developers / technical communities**: how it works and what was hard. Show HN wants a real technical angle and the maker answering questions, not marketing.
- **Startup / product directories**: outcome and who it replaces.
- **AI tool directories**: what it does, which models or tools it works with, how it is installed.

## Rules

- Lead with what someone can do now. Not "we're excited to announce".
- Specific beats superlative: a number, a time, a price, a named integration.
- Every number has a source and date. Numbers that belong to a partner, a protocol or a platform are labelled as theirs ("network-wide", "across the protocol").
- No guarantees about speed, returns, availability or outcomes you do not control. Put real caveats in the body, once, plainly.
- Demo modes, testnets and examples say so where the reader sees them.
- One call to action per piece.
- No hashtag clouds, no emoji confetti, no thread padding ("1/🧵").
- Run `deslop`'s scan over `copy.md` before anything goes out.

## Bad and good

> **Bad:** 🚀 Introducing Lumen, the revolutionary AI-powered platform that seamlessly transforms how teams work! ✨
>
> **Good:** Lumen turns a messy task list into one finish line: it groups related work, flags what blocks shipping, and tells you when you're done. Free for solo use. lumen.example

> **Bad (borrowed metric):** Over $50M traded!
>
> **Good:** Runs on the same payments platform that processed $50M last year (their figure, across all merchants); this app is new and small.
