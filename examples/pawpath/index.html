How it works

  1. Meet at your door

    A free 20-minute meet at home, so your dog meets their walker before the first walk.

  2. Out to the park

    A group of at most 4 dogs, or a solo walk. Collected from your door and returned to it.

  3. A photo and a note

    After every walk you get a photo and a short note by text.
