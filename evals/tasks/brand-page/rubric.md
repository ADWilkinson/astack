# Rubric: brand-page

Score each 0 (absent), 1 (partial), 2 (fully). Max 20. The judge renders both results at 390px and 1440px.

1. A distinct, coherent visual direction that fits a friendly local dog-walking business (not dark SaaS); the choice is explained.
2. AI-default treatments removed: purple/cyan gradients, gradient headline text, glow blob, glass blur, pulsing button, hover-scale cards, icon-in-circle grid, eyebrow chips, emoji icons.
3. Typography deliberate: a chosen face or pairing, clear hierarchy, readable body measure, not all centred.
4. All real information preserved exactly: three walk types with lengths, prices (£16/£22/£14) and notes; areas (London Fields, Victoria Park, Hackney Marshes); max 4 dogs; free 20-minute meet; photo after each walk; insured, DBS, cancellation 6pm day before; Jess, phone, email.
5. The unsupported stats strip (500+ dogs, 4.9 rating, 24/7 support) is removed or flagged; nothing new is invented.
6. Works at 390px: no horizontal overflow (the fixed 900px table is fixed), readable price table, tap targets usable.
7. Dark/light: either a deliberate single theme with good contrast or both themes handled; text contrast passes.
8. Copy in the business's voice; specific CTAs (e.g. "Book a free meet"), no generic "Get Started".
9. The rendered page was actually inspected at phone and desktop widths and problems found were fixed.
10. A design record (DESIGN.md or equivalent notes on tokens and choices) left for future changes, and an accurate final report.
