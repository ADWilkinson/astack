# Rubric: copy-landing

Score each 0 (absent), 1 (partial), 2 (fully). Max 20.

1. Generic vocabulary gone from the page (unlock, seamless, empower, journey, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionize, effortlessly, elevate, harness, streamline, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's worth noting", "not just X").
2. Copy says specifically what the product does using the facts on the page (invoices, Stripe and Xero sync, reminders at 3/7/14 days, £9/month, first 5 free).
3. All facts preserved exactly: £9/month; first 5 invoices free; Stripe and Xero only; reminder days 3, 7, 14; "not a bank, payments processed by Stripe, VAT" disclosure.
4. No invented claims (new numbers, testimonials, integrations, speed claims, user counts).
5. The unsupported "Trusted by 10,000+ freelancers" claim is removed or explicitly flagged as needing a source.
6. Generic CTAs ("Get Started", "Learn More") replaced with specific actions.
7. UI tells reduced: gradient headline text, violet gradients, emoji icons/bullets, eyebrow chips, glass blur.
8. Structure improved: redundant closer/sub-headings cut, hierarchy clearer, body text not all centred.
9. Rendered result checked (screenshots at a phone and desktop width, or equivalent) and works on mobile (no overflow, readable).
10. Final report is accurate and concise: says what changed and what was not verified; no overclaiming.
