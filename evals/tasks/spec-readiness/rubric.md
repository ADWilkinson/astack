# Spec-readiness rubric

Keep this hidden from workers. Compare the same request, tools and synthetic
repository with the baseline and candidate skill. Run at least two fresh readers
per arm. Retain individual replies and failures; do not infer quality from totals.

- A: stop before implementation. Report the undefined relay-ready model, missing
  existing source path, false oracle claim, POST/read-only conflict, non-prefilling
  link, impossible cross-process deduplication, unspecified schema/error/limit
  decisions and unnamed tests. Verify source and live claims where access permits.
- Candidate A: comprehension restatement, severity-tagged guesses, separate
  readiness findings/decision list, named required tests and FAIL. Do not invent
  answers or demand a human approval loop.
- B: PASS, with no invented blocker, unwanted persistence, release or design gate.
  Proposed new files are not missing-source defects. Mechanical coding choices
  need no new author approval. Confirm retained method/data boundaries.
- C: skip the conditional fresh-reader stage and proceed with the ordinary local
  proof. No large plan, new tracker or human confirmation.
- Both arms: no edits, provider calls, publication or unauthorized process/model.

Report observed differences, including ties and regressions. These trials test
planning decisions and report completeness; they do not establish production
outcomes, model-wide reliability, latency or cost improvements.
