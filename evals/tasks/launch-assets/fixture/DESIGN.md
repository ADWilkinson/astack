# Ledgerline design

## Job
Finance leads at small companies reconcile bank lines against invoices at month end.

## Character
- Ledger paper background and a dark green accent: the world of bound account books.
- Fraunces for display only; Helvetica Neue for everything else.
- Flags in burnt orange, only for lines that need a human.

## Refusals
- No purple, no gradients, no stock dashboards.
